Wally is a digital wallet for your things, like bank, loyalty & ID cards, passports & travel documents, event tickets, business cards, notes, receipts, photos, etc.

Wally protects your cards with biometric authentication, and can generate QR codes and barcodes for your cards, call and email your business contacts, etc.
