{{page.version}} is out, with support for custom fonts, a new denim skin, and a corduroy skin collection!

![Blog post header]({{page.header}})


## Custom Fonts

{{page.version}} lets you pick a custom font for navigation titles and decorative texts. You can pick between two hand-drawn fonts, a font for dyslexia, and all system fonts.


## New Skins

{{page.version}} adds a calm, red denim skin called Santa, as well as a brand new corduroy skin collection with four brand new skins - Corduroy, Brick, Chestnut and Pine.


## Simplified Menu Customizations

You can now customize which item types to show in the main menu, directly from the main menu. 


## Conclusion

You can [download {{page.version}} from the App Store]({{site.urls.appstore}}). We hope that you will love these changes, and hope that you reach out with any suggestions you may have.
