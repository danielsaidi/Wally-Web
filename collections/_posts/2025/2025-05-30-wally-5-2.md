{{page.version}} is out, with a new document scanner, a quick edit mode, and a polished onboarding flow!

![Blog post header]({{page.header}})


## Document Scanner

{{page.version}} lets you scan a document to automatically extract the subject from the image. This makes adding things to your wallet a lot easier than before.

No more manually rotating and trimming your photos (although you still can) - {{page.version}}'s document scanner is there to help speed things up.


## Quick Edit Mode

{{page.version}} lets you long press any item in your wallet to quickly open an edit screen directly from the main wallet screen. This saves a few taps when you want to add information or update images.


## Conclusion

You can [download {{page.version}} from the App Store]({{site.urls.appstore}}). We hope that you will love these changes, and hope that you reach out with any suggestions you may have.
