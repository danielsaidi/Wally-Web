{{page.version}} is being rolled out, with support for Liquid Glass and new date features, and with a lower subscription price that makes it even more attractive to upgrade to Premium!

![Blog post header]({{page.promo}})


## Liquid Glass

Wally 6 has been redesigned for Liquid Glass. The result is a lighter app that gives you more room to breathe, with more playful animations and transitions.


## Date Features

You can now set a date on your tickets, and an expiration and reminder date on your cards and other expirable items.


## Lower Subscription Price

Wally 6 will adjust the Premium subscription price from a $2.99/$29.99 to $1.99/$19.99. This will make it more attractive to upgrade for those of you who use the app less often.


## Conclusion

You can [download {{page.version}} from the App Store]({{site.urls.appstore}}) once it goes live. We hope that you will love the many changes in this version.
